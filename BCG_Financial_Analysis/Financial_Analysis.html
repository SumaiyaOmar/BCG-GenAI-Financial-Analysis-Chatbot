Financial Performance Analysis: Apple, Microsoft, and Tesla (2023–2025)¶

Project Overview¶

This project examines the financial performance of Apple, Microsoft, and Tesla from fiscal years 2023 to 2025 using Python and pandas.

Financial data was collected from the companies' annual Form 10-K reports filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC).

Project Objectives¶

  • Analyze year-over-year changes in revenue, net income, and operating cash flow.
  • Compare profitability and financial performance across the three companies.
  • Identify important financial trends using pandas and data visualizations.
  • Summarize findings to support data-driven financial insights.

Tools Used¶

  • Python
  • pandas
  • Matplotlib
  • Jupyter Notebook (Visual Studio Code)
  • Microsoft Excel

Note: Financial figures are expressed in USD millions unless otherwise specified.

Data Sources and Methodology¶

Data Sources¶

Financial data for Apple, Microsoft, and Tesla was manually collected from their annual Form 10-K reports available through the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) EDGAR database.

The dataset covers fiscal years 2023, 2024, and 2025 and includes the following financial metrics:

  • Total Revenue
  • Net Income
  • Total Assets
  • Total Liabilities
  • Operating Cash Flow

All financial values are recorded in USD millions.

Methodology¶

  1. Data Collection: Extracted financial figures from annual reports and organized them into a structured dataset using Microsoft Excel.
  2. Data Import: Loaded the dataset into a pandas DataFrame in Jupyter Notebook.
  3. Financial Calculations: Calculated year-over-year percentage changes in revenue, net income, and operating cash flow, along with net profit margins and liabilities-to-assets ratios.
  4. Trend Analysis: Compared financial performance across companies and fiscal years using pandas.
  5. Data Visualization: Created charts using Matplotlib to illustrate revenue trends, profitability, and operating cash flow.
  6. Aggregation: Used pandas groupby() and agg() functions to calculate average financial performance for each company.
  7. Interpretation: Summarized significant trends, differences, and financial findings.

Financial Metrics and Year-over-Year Analysis¶

To evaluate financial performance, additional metrics were calculated using pandas.

  • Revenue Growth (%): Measures the percentage change in revenue compared with the previous fiscal year.
  • Net Income Growth (%): Measures the percentage change in net income compared with the previous fiscal year.
  • Net Profit Margin (%): Measures the percentage of revenue retained as net income.
  • Liabilities to Assets (%): Measures the proportion of total assets represented by liabilities.
  • Operating Cash Flow Growth (%): Measures the percentage change in cash generated from operating activities.

The groupby() and pct_change() functions were used to calculate year-over-year growth separately for each company.

Note: Growth values for 2023 are unavailable (NaN) because the dataset does not include financial data for 2022.

Company-Level Financial Summary (2023–2025)¶

To compare the overall financial performance of the three companies, pandas groupby() and agg() functions were used to calculate average financial metrics across the three fiscal years.

The summary includes average total revenue, net income, net profit margin, and operating cash flow.

Key Observations¶

  • Apple: Recorded the highest average total revenue and average net income.
  • Microsoft: Maintained the highest average net profit margin, demonstrating strong profitability relative to revenue.
  • Tesla: Recorded the lowest average revenue, net income, and operating cash flow among the three companies.

These results provide a company-level comparison that complements the year-over-year trend analysis.

Note: The averages are based on three fiscal years (2023–2025), and financial values are expressed in USD millions, except for net profit margin (%).

Financial Analysis: Key Findings (2023–2025)¶

1. Revenue Performance¶

Apple generated the highest total revenue among the three companies throughout the analysis period. However, Microsoft demonstrated the strongest revenue growth between 2023 and 2025, while Tesla experienced a slight overall decline.

2. Profitability¶

Microsoft maintained the highest average net profit margin at 35.42%, compared with Apple's 25.40% and Tesla's 8.95%. This indicates that Microsoft was the most profitable relative to revenue over the three-year period.

3. Operating Cash Flow¶

Microsoft demonstrated substantial growth in operating cash flow, increasing from $87,582 million in 2023 to $136,162 million in 2025. Apple maintained relatively stable operating cash flow, while Tesla operated at considerably lower cash flow levels.

4. Overall Financial Trends¶

Microsoft demonstrated strong revenue growth, consistent profitability, and increasing operating cash flow. Apple maintained the largest revenue base and substantial profitability, while Tesla experienced declining net income and weakening profit margins.

Conclusion¶

Based on the financial metrics examined, Microsoft demonstrated the strongest overall growth and profitability trends during 2023–2025. Apple maintained a strong financial position in terms of revenue and earnings, while Tesla showed signs of declining profitability that may warrant further investigation.
